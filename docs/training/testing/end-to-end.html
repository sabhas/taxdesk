End-to-end walkthrough: a day at the firm

One client's annual return, from first contact to the money in the bank, handled by five people. Run it as a group exercise; it takes 60–90 minutes. It uses the accounts from Build a training environment.

1. A new client walks in t.manager

  1. Clients: search "Ayesha Training". No results. Select New client.
  2. Individual, legal name Ayesha Training, CNIC 00000-0000001-1, assigned manager t.consultant, primary contact with an @example.test email. Select Check and create client.
  3. Check: a CL-… code is assigned, and the CNIC shows masked.
  4. Try to create a second client with the same CNIC. Check: This client already exists blocks it.

2. Open the case t.consultant

  1. Sign in as the consultant. Check: Ayesha appears under Clients (you are her manager).
  2. On her page, select Open case: service "Income tax return", tax year 2026, you as primary consultant, t.reviewer as reviewer.
  3. Check: the due date is pre-filled 30 days ahead, the case opens in Intake, and it appears under My work.

3. Collect documents t.consultant

  1. Move the status to Collecting documents.
  2. Try to move straight to Filed. Check: there is no such button (it is not an allowed move).
  3. Upload document: the sample CNIC PDF, checklist item "Client identity document", type "CNIC copy". Upload a bank statement and a salary certificate the same way.
  4. Upload the large file, select Pause, then Resume. Check: it completes.

4. Try to complete too early t.consultant

  1. Move to In preparation.
  2. Select Complete on "Client identity document". Check: refused with "Link and verify a CNIC copy document before completing this item."
  3. Check: the preparer cannot verify documents on their own case; that is the reviewer's (or a manager's) job.
  4. Move to In review.

5. Review the documents t.reviewer

  1. Sign in as the reviewer. Check: the case is in My work.
  2. Verify the CNIC copy and the bank statement. Reject the salary certificate with the reason "Wrong year".
  3. Move the case back to In preparation.

6. Complete, review, and file

  1. t.consultant completes "Client identity document" and "Bank statements", then uploads a corrected salary certificate.
  2. t.reviewer verifies the new salary certificate.
  3. t.consultant completes "Income evidence" and moves the case to In review.
  4. t.reviewer selects Return on "Bank statements" with the comment "Add the savings account". Check: Filed is greyed out with the reason shown.
  5. t.consultant completes "Bank statements" again.
  6. t.reviewer verifies "Bank statements" and "Income evidence", then moves the case to Filed.
  7. Check: the checklist is now locked, and the status history shows every move with who and when.
Separation of duties

Whoever completed an item can never verify it, not even a manager. That is what makes the review meaningful.

7. Bill the work t.billing

  1. Open the case and select Create invoice. Check: the first line is "Income tax return" at 25,000.
  2. Add a line "Advisory call", quantity 1, 5,000. Discount 2,000. Save draft. Check: total 28,000.
  3. Finalize… → Finalize invoice. Check: it gets INV-…, and Download PDF works.

8. Take payments t.billing

  1. Record payment: 10,000, Cash. Check: Partly paid, outstanding 18,000, receipt RCP-….
  2. Try to record 20,000. Check: refused as more than outstanding.
  3. Record 18,000 by Bank transfer. Check: Paid.
  4. Check: there is no Reverse button for the Billing role.

9. Correct a mistake t.manager

  1. The 18,000 was recorded in error. On that payment, select Reverse, amount 18,000, reason "Bank transfer bounced".
  2. Check: back to Partly paid, and both the payment and the reversal appear in the history.
  3. Move the case to Completed (as manager).

10. Look back admin

  1. Dashboard: check the billing figures and filing progress, and click a number to confirm it lists exactly those rows.
  2. Reports → Billing, this month: invoiced 28,000, reversed 18,000.
  3. Audit history, action billing.*: finalize, payments, and reversal, each with actor and request ID. Search the case ID to see its whole story.
  4. Archive the case. Check: it leaves the queues, is read-only, and still has its history.
Discussion questions for the class
  • Why could the consultant not verify their own work?
  • Why does a manager reverse payments, but not record them?
  • What would you do if a document showed "Missing in storage"?
  • Where would you look to find out who changed a client's phone number last week?
