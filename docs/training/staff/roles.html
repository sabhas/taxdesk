Roles and permissions

TaxDesk has five fixed roles. An administrator gives each user a role on the Users page. There is no custom-role editor.

The five roles

Administrator

Everything: users, settings, document storage, data import, audit history, and every record.

Manager

Sees all clients and cases, manages clients, services, and workflows, assigns work, reviews. Reads billing and can reverse payments, but cannot create invoices or record payments.

Consultant

Works on assigned cases: checklists and documents. Sees only clients they manage and cases assigned to them. No billing or reports.

Billing

Invoices, payments, receipts, voids, and billing reports. Sees all clients and cases but not documents. Cannot reverse payments.

Read-only

Views clients, cases, documents, invoices, reports, and the dashboard. Changes nothing.

Hidden is not the same as protected

The app hides menu items and buttons your role cannot use, but the server checks every request again. Typing a URL or guessing a record ID never reveals data. A record you may not see and a record that does not exist get the same "access denied" answer, so guessing an ID confirms nothing.

Choosing a role for someone

  • Partners and office heads: Manager. Give Administrator to two trusted people only.
  • Tax consultants and preparers: Consultant.
  • Accounts and front desk taking payments: Billing.
  • Auditors, interns, and observers: Read-only.

Give the smallest role that lets the person do their job. Changing a role signs the user out everywhere, and the change appears in audit history.
