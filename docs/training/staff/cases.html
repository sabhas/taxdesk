Tax cases

A case is one piece of work: one client, one service, one tax year. It moves through the statuses of its workflow, and its checklist proves the work was prepared and reviewed.

Work queues

Cases opens on work queues. "Due dates use the firm calendar", so "today" means today in the firm's timezone.

Queue Shows

Due soonOpen cases due today or within the next 7 days
OverduePast the due date and still open

Each queue shows a count, which always matches its list. Filter by Service, Status key (for example in_review), Primary consultant, Reviewer, and Tax year. Columns: Case, Client, Service, Status, Due, Consultant, Reviewer.

Open a case

  1. Select New case on the Cases page, or Open case on a client's page (the client is pre-filled).
  2. Choose the Client, Service, Tax year, and Workflow version. The Due date is pre-filled from the service's deadline; change it if needed.
  3. Choose the Primary consultant (required) and an optional Reviewer, who must be a different person. Consultants can only name themselves as primary; managers can assign anyone.
  4. Add notes and select Open case.

The case gets a code such as TC-00042 and copies the workflow version's statuses and checklist.

Possible duplicate case

If the same client already has an open case for the same service and tax year, either you get Possible duplicate case and must give a Reason to open another case before Open case anyway, or the case is refused, depending on the service's policy.

The case page

  • Summary: client, service, tax year, due date with an overdue or due-soon flag, fee, workflow version, consultant, and reviewer. Edit details changes the due date, assignees, and notes.
  • Status: the current status and a button for each allowed next status.
  • Checklist: items with Required, Optional, and Needs review badges, plus counts.
  • Documents and Invoices for this case.
  • Status history (permanent: who, when, from, to, note) and Activity (edits, assignments, checklist decisions).

Moving the status

Select the button for the next status and add a note if you like. The server checks four things together: the case is still in the status you saw, the move is allowed by the workflow, you have permission, and the checklist conditions are met.

  • Moves that are not possible yet are greyed out, with the reason shown underneath (for example, required checklist items are not complete).
  • The status can only change through these buttons. Nothing else can alter it.

The checklist: preparer and reviewer

  • The primary consultant (preparer) selects Complete or Reopen.
  • The reviewer selects Verify, or Return (Return item to preparer, with a required comment).
  • Nobody can verify their own work. Whoever completed an item cannot verify it, even a manager. Managers can also complete and verify items on any case.
  • If an item names a document type and is required, it can only be completed once a verified document is linked to it. If that document is later rejected, deleted, or goes missing, the item reopens automatically.
  • Filed and Completed need every required item complete, and verified where review is required.
  • Once the case is filed, the checklist locks.

When a colleague got there first

If someone changed the case, moved its status, or decided a checklist item after you loaded the page, your action is refused instead of overwriting theirs. The page explains the conflict and reloads the latest version. Look at what changed, then try again if it still makes sense.

Archive

Managers and administrators can archive completed or cancelled cases (Archive, then Archive case). Archived cases leave the working queues, become read-only, and keep all history and documents. Open cases cannot be archived; cancel them first.

A consultant's day

  1. Open the Dashboard: assigned, overdue, due soon, checklist gaps.
  2. Work Overdue, then Due soon, then My work.
  3. Upload documents against checklist items and complete items.
  4. Move the case to In review when it is ready, and fix anything the reviewer returns.
