Dashboard and reports

The dashboard tells you what needs attention today. Reports answer questions over a period and export to CSV.

The dashboard

Dashboard greets you with "Welcome, <name>" and shows only the sections your role can use. Every number is a link to a list showing exactly those rows.

Section Figures Who sees it
Cases that need attention Assigned to me, Overdue, "Due within N days" (the firm's due-soon window), With checklist gaps, Filing progress (In progress / Filed / Completed) Everyone who can read cases
Team workload Unassigned, Open team cases, Overdue team cases, Review queue, and a per-staff table (open, overdue, due soon, reviewing) Administrator Manager
Billing Unpaid invoices, Overdue invoices, Collected this month (net of reversals) Administrator Manager Billing Read-only
Account security, Your access Your MFA state and your role's permissions Everyone

"This month" and "overdue" use the firm's timezone.

Reports

Reports has three tabs. Results are paged. Consultant has no access.

Tab Filters Shows CSV
Cases Due-date range, service, status, staff member, tax year, active/archived Matching cases Export CSV
Staff workload — Open, overdue, due-soon, and reviewing counts per person —
Billing Date range Invoiced, Payments received, Reversed, Net collected, Outstanding, Overdue Export CSV
  • Billing totals always match the invoice pages; they come from the same figures.
  • Every export is recorded in audit history. Exported files contain client data: keep them on firm devices and delete them when done.
  • If nothing matches, the report says so. It does not show an empty table without explanation.

Questions the reports answer

What's overdue this week, and whose is it?Dashboard → Overdue, or Cases report filtered by due date
Who is overloaded?Staff workload
How many 2026 returns are filed?Cases report: tax year 2026, status Filed
How much did we collect last month?Billing tab, last month's date range: Net collected
Who owes us money?Invoices list, Payment = Unpaid or Partly paid
