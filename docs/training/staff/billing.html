Invoice life cycle

Create and edit a draft

  1. Open the case (or client) and select Create invoice. From a case, the first line is the service name and its fee. From a client, the draft starts empty. Invoices are only created this way, not from the Invoices list.
  2. Edit line items (Description, Quantity, Unit amount (PKR)). Use Add line, move lines up or down, or remove them.
  3. Set the Discount (PKR), the Issue date (leave it empty to use the finalize date), the Due date, and Notes.
  4. Select Save draft. The totals on screen are a preview; the server recalculates them on save. Negative totals are refused.

Combined invoice for a client group

A group head can be billed with one invoice that covers work for the head and its members. The head receives and pays the invoice. You can still bill each member on its own instead.

  1. Open the group head's client page and select Create combined invoice (next to Create invoice). It is offered only on a client that heads a group, and the draft starts empty with no case of its own.
  2. Select Add line. Each line has Billed for (the group head or an active member) and an optional Case (optional) from that client's cases. Choosing a case fills the line with the service name, tax year, and service fee; you can still change them.
  3. If the case is already on another invoice that is not void, a warning names that invoice, for example "TC-00009 is already on INV-00003 (finalized). You can still bill it here, for example for follow-up work." It never blocks you.
  4. The total preview shows a subtotal for each client. Save and finalize as usual.
  • At finalize, TaxDesk checks again that every line's client is still the head or a member. If a member has left the group, the finalize dialog closes, the line's Billed for field is marked, and you see "CL-00002 is no longer in this group. Change or remove its lines, then finalize." The same happens on Save draft if a line's client or case is refused. The mark clears as soon as you edit the draft.
  • The finalized invoice keeps each line's client code and name as they were. Renaming a client or changing the group later never changes the invoice or its PDF.
  • The PDF is addressed to the head. It groups the lines under each client's name with a subtotal for each, then the discount and total. It shows names only, never a CNIC or NTN.
  • Payments, receipts, reversals, and void work on the whole invoice, exactly as for any other invoice. Amounts are not split between members.
  • You see a combined invoice only if you can see every client on it. The Invoices list marks it combined for the group, and the invoice page shows a Combined badge.

Finalize

Select Finalize…, then Finalize invoice in Finalize this invoice?. The invoice:

  • gets the next number, with no gaps (INV-00001, INV-00002, …), even if two people finalize at the same moment;
  • is frozen: lines and amounts can never change;
  • gets a PDF stored in document storage, available from Download PDF. The PDF carries the firm logo when an administrator has set one (see Firm logo); receipts do too.

If you clicked twice or the network retried, you still get one invoice and one number. If storage was down, the invoice is saved anyway and shows PDF could not be stored. Select Generate PDF again later.

Record a payment

  1. On the invoice, select Record payment.
  2. In Record a payment, enter the Amount (PKR), Method (Cash, Bank transfer, Cheque, Card, Other), Received on, and an optional Reference (for example a cheque number).
  3. Save. The balance updates and a receipt (RCP-00001) with its own PDF is created.
  • Partial payments are fine. The state becomes Partly paid.
  • Zero, negative, and more-than-outstanding amounts are refused, even when two people pay at the same time.
  • Submitting the same payment twice (double click, retry) records it once.
  • Download a receipt with Receipt on the payment row.

Reverse a payment

Administrator Manager only. Use this when a payment was recorded by mistake or a cheque bounced.

  1. On the payment row, select Reverse.
  2. Enter the Amount to reverse (PKR) (all or part) and a Reason.

The original payment stays in the history, and a linked reversal entry is added. You cannot reverse more than was paid, even with two reversals at once.

Void an invoice

Void invoice is allowed for a draft, or for a finalized invoice with no net payments (reverse any payments first). Give a reason and confirm in Void this invoice?. Voiding is permanent. The invoice stays visible with its number and history. To fix a wrong finalized invoice: void it, then create a new one.

Finding invoices

Invoices lists every invoice ("Invoices and what is still owed"). Filter by Status (Draft, Finalized, Void), Payment, and Group. Columns: Invoice, Client, Due, Total, Outstanding, Status. Case and client pages show their own invoices. The invoice page shows Issue date, Due date, Subtotal, Discount, Total, Paid, Outstanding, the line items, the PDF, and Payments and receipts.

Billing for a client group

Choose a group head in the Group filter, or select Group billing on the group head's client page. The list shows the invoices of the head and its members, and three totals appear above it: Invoiced, Paid, and Outstanding. The totals count finalized invoices only; drafts and void invoices are left out. A combined invoice counts against the group head (and so the group), never against a member. A case's invoice list also shows combined invoices that bill that case on one of their lines.

Billing never moves a case

Paying or voiding an invoice does not change the case's status, and the other way round.

Money rules to remember

  • Finalized invoices, payments, and reversals are never edited or deleted.
  • Corrections are always new entries: a reversal, or a void plus a new invoice.
  • Numbers never have gaps and are never reused. A changed prefix applies only to new numbers.
