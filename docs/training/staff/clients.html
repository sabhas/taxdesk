Clients

The client record is the anchor for cases, documents, and invoices. TaxDesk protects identifiers, stops duplicates, and never lets two people silently overwrite each other.

Administrator Manager create and edit · Billing Read-only view all · Consultant views the clients they manage

Find a client

Open Clients. The search box accepts a client code (CL-00001), a name, a phone number, an email, or an exact CNIC/NTN.

  • Name search ignores capitals, punctuation, and company suffixes, so "khan traders" finds "Khan Traders (Pvt.) Ltd.".
  • Phone numbers match in national or international form (0300… or 92300…).
  • Filters: Type (Individual, Business), Manager (Any manager, Unassigned, or a person), Group (Any group, or a group head: shows the head and its members), Status (Active, Archived, Active and archived). Select Search.
  • Columns: Client, Type, Identifier (masked), Primary contact, Manager, Group, Status. The Group column names the group head a client is part of, or shows Group head · 2 members on a head (select it to list the group). Use Load more clients to page through.
  • Consultants see the note "You see the clients assigned to you as manager."

Create a client

  1. Select New client.
  2. Choose the Client type. It cannot be changed later.
    • Individual: CNIC required (13 digits), NTN optional.
    • Business: NTN required, no CNIC.
  3. Enter the Legal name (required), an optional Display name, and the Assigned manager (the person responsible; for consultants this decides what they can see).
  4. Optionally choose a Parent client to put the client in a group (see Client groups).
  5. Add up to 10 contacts (Name, Relationship, Email, Phone) with Add contact. Mark exactly one as Primary contact.
  6. Add notes if useful, then select Check and create client.

TaxDesk gives the client a code such as CL-00042. Codes are never reused.

Same name, email, or phone This client may already exist Managers and administrators can fill in Reason for creating a separate client and select Create anyway. The reason is kept in the client's history.

How CNIC and NTN are protected

  • They are encrypted in the database.
  • They are always shown masked, for example •••••-••••567-1. There is no "show full number" button in this release.
  • Activity history records that an identifier changed, never its value.
  • Search finds a client by an exact identifier without revealing it.

Edit a client and handle conflicts

Open the client and select Edit, change what you need, then Save changes.

If a colleague saved the same client while you were editing, you see Someone else saved this client, with your unsaved input beside the latest version. Choose one:

  • Discard my changes and use the latest
  • Keep reviewing my changes
  • Save my changes over the latest. Only do this after reading what your colleague changed.

Client groups

A group is a group head (a client, for example a business) and the clients linked to it, such as the owner's family or business partners. Link a client to its head with Parent client on the client's New client or Edit form, then Save changes. Choose None to remove the link.

  • Only managers and administrators can set or change a parent client. The list offers active clients that are not themselves part of a group.
  • Groups are one level deep. A client cannot be its own parent, a client that is part of a group cannot be a parent, and a group head cannot be given a parent. On a group head's edit form the field is switched off and explains why. If a colleague made a conflicting change at the same moment, you see "This parent client is not allowed. Groups are one level deep…"; reload and choose again.
  • Archiving a group head or a member keeps the link. An archived client cannot be chosen as a new parent until it is restored.
  • The client page shows Part of on a member, and a Group members card on a head, with links to Group clients, Group cases, and Group billing.
  • The Group filter is also on Cases and Invoices. On Invoices it shows the group's invoiced, paid, and outstanding totals.
  • A group never widens what you can see. Consultants see only the members they manage; other members are not listed or counted.
  • The client's activity shows Changed the parent client; the audit log keeps the old and new parent client codes.

The client page

It shows the Summary, Contacts, and Notes, and also:

  • Part of (members) or Group members (group heads), see Client groups
  • Cases, with Open case to start a new one for this client
  • Invoices, with Create invoice (Billing and Administrator)
  • Documents, for client-level files (see Documents)
  • Activity: who created, changed, archived, or restored the client, and which fields changed. Use Show older activity for more.

Archive and restore

Archive, confirmed with Archive client, hides the client from the default list and makes it read-only. Nothing is deleted. Restore (Restore client) brings it back. Archived clients still block duplicates of their CNIC/NTN.

Good habits

  • Search before creating. Most duplicates are caught before you start typing.
  • Always set an assigned manager, otherwise the client appears as "Unassigned".
  • Keep notes factual. Never paste a full CNIC or a password into notes.
