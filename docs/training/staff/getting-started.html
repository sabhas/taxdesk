Getting started and MFA

Everyone starts here. Activate your account, set up your authenticator app, keep your recovery codes safe, and learn how sessions work.

What you need

  • An activation link from your firm administrator. TaxDesk never emails it; the administrator gives it to you directly.
  • An authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, or Authy.
  • A safe place for ten recovery codes: the firm's password vault, or a printed copy in a locked drawer.

1. Activate your account

  1. Open the link. It works once and expires 30 minutes after the administrator created it.
  2. On Activate your TaxDesk account, enter a New password (at least 12 characters) and Confirm password.
  3. Select Activate account, then Go to sign in.

If the link has expired or was already used, ask the administrator for a new one. It takes them a few seconds.

2. Sign in

On Sign in to TaxDesk, enter your username (not an email address) and your password, then select Sign in. Your username is permanent and was chosen by your administrator.

If the details are wrong, TaxDesk deliberately does not say which part was wrong. After several failures you may be asked to complete a short "are you human" check (Cloudflare Turnstile). After more failures, sign-in is paused for a few minutes: "Too many sign-in attempts. Wait a few minutes and try again."

3. Set up multi-factor authentication (first sign-in)

MFA is mandatory. You cannot open any firm record until it is set up.

  1. On Set up multi-factor authentication, open your authenticator app and add an account.
  2. Scan the QR code. If you cannot scan, use Use a setup link instead or copy the secret into the app.
  3. Type the Six-digit code the app shows and select Enable MFA.
  4. Save your recovery codes. Ten codes are shown once. Store them safely, then select I have saved these codes.

4. Signing in every day

  1. Enter your username and password.
  2. On Multi-factor verification, type the current code from your app and select Verify. You have 5 minutes; after that, select Start over.
  3. No phone? Switch to Recovery code and type one of your saved codes. Each recovery code works once.
Codes rejected even though they look right?

Your phone's clock is probably wrong. Turn on automatic date and time, then try again.

Sessions and signing out

Idle timeout 30 minutes without activity signs you out
Maximum session 12 hours, then you sign in again, even if you were active
Sign out Sign out at the bottom of the side menu
Expired session "Your session expired. Sign in again to continue." The page you were on opens again after you sign in.
Forced sign-out Happens when an administrator changes your role, resets you, or deactivates you, and when your own password or MFA changes

Every screen has its own address, so you can bookmark pages and use the browser's back and forward buttons. On a phone, open the menu with Open navigation.

The Security page

Open Security in the side menu. You can:

  • Regenerate recovery codes: enter your password and a current authenticator code, then select Regenerate codes. Old codes stop working, and your other sessions are signed out. Do this when you have used most of your codes, or think someone has seen them.
  • Disable MFA: enter your password and a code, tick I understand that MFA will be removed from my account., and select Disable MFA. You must set up MFA again before you can use TaxDesk. Use this when moving to a new phone.

Lost your phone or forgot your password?

Problem What to do
New phone, old phone still works Security → disable MFA, then enrol again on the new phone
Phone lost, recovery codes available Sign in with a recovery code, then disable and re-enrol MFA on the Security page
Phone and codes both lost Ask an administrator. They will arrange an MFA reset.
Forgot password Ask an administrator for a Reset link. It works once, within 30 minutes.
Correct details are still refused (your account may be deactivated) Ask an administrator
